## Known properties of the inputs
- Plants run (trnL) taxonomy: 1,854 of 3,898 ASVs (8.9% of reads; up to 29% in some libraries) have no genus, and 24 logged plant foods have a genus absent from every taxonomy file of the run (e.g. corn *Zea*, apple *Malus*, cucumber/melon *Cucumis*, pumpkin/squash *Cucurbita*, strawberry *Fragaria*), so they cannot be matched; most likely gaps in the trnL reference used for assignment, not lexicon naming errors (component 1, section 0.2b).
- Whole-week agreement (component 1, section 0.9, 68 food taxa, detection >= 0.1%, all subjects pooled): stool recall 0.55, precision 0.58; capsule recall 0.78, precision 0.68; saliva recall 0.74, precision 0.50.
- Questionnaire vs diet log (notebook section 0.9, 2026-10-18, descriptive): fermented-food frequency agrees only loosely with the logged week (Spearman 0.39 for live-microbe foods, 0.23 for `Fermented? = Yes`; 6 and 2 of 19 subjects inside the range their answer implies). Counting `Variable` foods as well lowers the agreement (0.28 and 0.16; 4 and 5 of 19 inside). Dairy frequency agrees better (Spearman 0.57; 10 of 19 inside; with protein powder 0.56, 11 of 19). The self-reported vegan (04) logged no animal foods; the two vegetarians (03, 20) logged dairy, egg and assumed gelatin but no meat or fish.
- Daily-Nutrient: for most subjects it covers the days before the diet log started, not the study week; only some subjects have nutrient data across the logged days.
- The sample sheet's `16S_filename` does not match the 16S library names; libraries are mapped to `Sample_ID` through the `1-data` info sheets.
- Habitual intake (Pre-ingestion-Lifestyle): one answer per subject for dairy and fermented-food frequency, so only between-subject comparisons are possible.
- Earlier food-sequencing work (now in `old_scripts_figures/`) found food DNA in stool from ~12 h to ~5 days after eating, and that most plant ASVs are labelled only to genus.
